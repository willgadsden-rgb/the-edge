How to read it

Form L — the horse's expected run today in lengths against the typical winner of this class at this level of track (metro, provincial, country). +2.0L means it rates two lengths better than a typical winner. Measured from the history database, not set by hand.

Ceiling — its best relevant recent run on the same scale. A big gap between Form and Ceiling means it can run well above its average.

Conf — High = at least three effective recent runs and two on today's going and trip. Low = treat the rating as a guess.

Prep — run number this preparation from Form King. ★ means its record is best at today's stage (2+ runs there, 1+ point above its average).

Map — expected settling position from its real in-run positions (1 = leader). Debutants sit mid-field until they have form.

Model / True Odds — the model's win chance, then blended with the de-vigged market (70% market, 30% model) into a 100% book. True Odds = 1 ÷ that chance.

Edge / Min / Bet — edge is the live price (after 8% commission) against True Odds. Min = True Odds + 15%. A ✅ needs edge 20%+, the price at or above Min, and odds between $2.20 and $18.

Prices only change when you press Refresh prices (5 Form King credits a meeting) or turn on Auto prices. Scores change when the engine runs: about 9am and 11:30pm daily, or when you press Sync.
